DISASTER TAPE

Every disaster leaves a record.

I'm Phillip. I make documentaries about disasters with my animator, built from the records: inquiry reports, testimony, court papers and the photographs that survive.

Where the accounts disagree, the films say so. The reconstructions only show what the evidence supports.

The films are published on YouTube at youtube.com/@thedisastertape.
